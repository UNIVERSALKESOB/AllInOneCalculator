## en-US

All-in-One Calculator

**Short description (max 80)**

**Full description (max 4000)**
All-in-One Calculator puts ten everyday calculators in a single 65 KB app — no ads, no account, no tracking.

• Basic calculator — large keypad, live result preview, history of the last 50 calculations, and smart percentages (type 200 + 10% and get 220).
• Scientific mode — sin, cos, tan, logs, square root, powers, factorials, π and e, with a DEG/RAD switch.
• Unit converter — 60+ units across length, weight, temperature, area, volume, speed, time and data.
• Currency converter — 28 currencies with live rates; the last rates are cached so it keeps working offline.
• BMI — body mass index, weight category and the healthy weight range for your height.
• Loan / EMI — monthly installment, total interest and total repayment.
• Age — exact years, months and days, total days lived, and the countdown to your next birthday.
• Percentage — X% of Y, X is what percent of Y, and percentage increase/decrease.
• Tip — tip amount, total bill and per-person split, with 5–25% presets.
• Discount — final price after discount and tax, plus how much you save.

Everything is computed on your device. The app asks for internet access only to refresh currency rates, and it works without a connection.

Small, fast, private. Published by Universal Studio.
